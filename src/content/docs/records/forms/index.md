These records reproduce the supplied blank templates as searchable, accessible Markdown. Empty cells and ruled writing areas are identified as blank response areas; no form is completed.

The source forms use legacy Level I-V markings. Archive metadata explicitly normalizes those levels as I → TL-1, II → TL-2, III → TL-3, IV → TL-4, and V → TL-5 while preserving every original form marking verbatim.
