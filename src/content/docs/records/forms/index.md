Use the current approved form for each activity. Form titles, field schedules, and reference copies
are displayed only when the staff session satisfies the assigned information marking.

Legacy Level I–V markings remain in force as TL-1 through TL-5 for records-control purposes. The
original marking remains part of every issued form.

## Forms available to the base staff session

- [TL-101 — Routine Research & Technical Activity Record](/records/forms/tl-101/)
- [TL-220 — Controlled Experimental Study Record](/records/forms/tl-220/)
- [TL-P110 — Personnel Research Assignment Record](/records/forms/tl-p110/)
- [TL-O205 — Temporary Operational Order](/records/forms/tl-o205/)
- [TL-SOP-720 — Remote Field Expedition](/records/forms/tl-sop-720/)

## Controlled forms

Additional forms are listed in [Staff Services](/portal/forms/) according to the information
eligibility established for the current session. Request temporary access from the applicable
control record when assigned work requires a TL-3 or TL-4 form.
