Credential references are blank designs only; no issued identity credentials or completed personnel records appear in this archive.

## Staff workflow

An accepted staff session provides portal navigation and ordinary TL-1/TL-2 eligibility. Elevated information access remains separately authorized, time-limited, and scoped to the applicable record, program, or compartment. Physical authority never implies information eligibility.

## Authority model

Trinity uses two separate control systems:

1. **Information classification** establishes eligibility to receive information.
2. **Physical security authority** establishes where a person may go and what they may physically interact with.

Neither system automatically grants the authority represented by the other. See the [security reference index](/records/security/) for the controlling source language.
