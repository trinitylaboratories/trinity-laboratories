This system assigns an institutional oversight level to every article, specimen, organism, person,
device, environment, signal, process, or unresolved subject retained for surveillance, observation,
testing, housing, or study by Trinity Labs. The assigned level states the minimum control,
monitoring, documentation, and review required for the subject.

## Purpose and authority

Research-subject classification is not a security clearance, information classification, hazard
label, or permission to interact. Personnel must separately possess the required information
eligibility, facility authority, program authorization, and task approval.

Classification is determined by the most restrictive verified property, not by the subject's value,
secrecy, or perceived importance. A subject may move upward or downward only after documented
review.

> **Control rule:** Review every determinant independently. The highest level supported by any
> verified property governs. Do not average indicators or reduce a level because another category
> appears ordinary.

## Standard oversight levels

Color may support identification, but the printed code and title control.

| Level | Title               | Institutional meaning                                                                                                                                                | Minimum oversight                                                       |
| ----- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RS-0  | Reference           | Verified standard, control, archive item, or inactive subject with established identity and no dedicated surveillance requirement.                                   | Inventory and annual or closeout review.                                |
| RS-1  | Routine             | Conventional subject with known behavior, accepted handling methods, and no unusual dependency on observation or control conditions.                                 | Scheduled observation under an approved protocol.                       |
| RS-2  | Active Study        | Subject under current testing, repeated observation, or longitudinal study requiring a dedicated record and assigned research custodian.                             | Per-session record and monthly classification review.                   |
| RS-3  | Controlled          | Handling, transport, proximity, environmental conditions, or observer conduct can materially alter the subject, result, or reliability of the study.                 | Written control envelope, variance logging, and 14-day review.          |
| RS-4  | Condition-Dependent | Subject stability, safe retention, or data validity depends on continuously maintained environmental, mechanical, biological, behavioral, or operational conditions. | Continuous or near-continuous condition assurance and seven-day review. |

A high standard level may reflect severe chemical, biological, mechanical, medical, environmental,
or operational requirements without any nonstandard scientific finding.

### RS-P — Pending Review

RS-P is a temporary intake designation for an unidentified, newly recovered, or insufficiently
assessed subject. It does not establish a standing level or make a finding about the subject's
character or risk.

- Secure the subject in place when practical.
- Open a provisional record and assign a custodian.
- Apply no less than RS-3 handling during initial assessment.
- Replace RS-P with a standing level within 24 hours or by the end of the next staffed review
  period. Any delay requires written Laboratory Control approval.

## Classification determinants

The final level is the highest level supported by any verified determinant.

| Determinant                      | Routine evidence                                                                                  | Escalation handling                                                                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity and provenance          | Identity, origin, ownership, and chain of custody are independently supported.                    | Refer unresolved identity or custody exceptions for elevated review.                               |
| Predictability and repeatability | Responses are consistent with accepted models and repeat under similar conditions.                | Refer unexplained or unreproducible responses for elevated review.                                 |
| Control dependency               | Normal storage and handling are sufficient; brief interruption has limited effect.                | Reassess when retention or valid observation depends on maintained limits or active support.       |
| Consequence of deviation         | Deviation produces a limited, recoverable effect within the assigned workspace.                   | Elevated review is required when credible consequences exceed the assigned workspace.              |
| Mobility and propagation         | The subject remains localized and transfer can be controlled through ordinary custody procedures. | Elevated review is required when ordinary custody controls cannot maintain the assigned boundary.  |
| Observation and record integrity | Independent observers and instruments produce reconcilable records.                               | Refer any unresolved finding under this determinant to the controlling office for elevated review. |

## Assignment, review, and downgrade

| Step | Action         | Requirement                                                                                                                                  |
| ---- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Intake         | Open a subject record, assign a designator and custodian, and apply RS-P when a standing level is not yet supportable.                       |
| 2    | Initial review | The case lead and Laboratory Control reviewer compare the subject against every determinant and select the highest applicable level.         |
| 3    | Concurrence    | Obtain the independent concurrence or Directorate authority required by the selected level.                                                  |
| 4    | Marking        | Apply the current code to the primary record, container or boundary, monitoring station, transfer paperwork, and active test plan.           |
| 5    | Review         | Reassess at the default cycle and immediately after an incident, transfer, unexplained deviation, support failure, or material new evidence. |
| 6    | Downgrade      | A lower level requires two review signatures, resolved exceptions, and evidence that the lower controls remain adequate.                     |
| 7    | Closure        | Closing a study changes operational status; it does not erase classification, disposition requirements, or unresolved record obligations.    |

Every assignment, escalation, downgrade, and closure must identify the evidence reviewed,
unresolved exceptions, decision authority, effective time, and next review requirement.

## Default review cycles

The listed cycle is the maximum interval. A control order, active protocol, incident, facility
condition, or new evidence may require immediate or more frequent review.

| Level | Title               | Maximum routine interval                                             |
| ----- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RS-0  | Reference           | Annual inventory or formal closeout.                                 |
| RS-1  | Routine             | Every 90 days or at protocol completion.                             |
| RS-2  | Active Study        | Every 30 days and after any material protocol change.                |
| RS-3  | Controlled          | Every 14 days and after any unexplained variance.                    |
| RS-4  | Condition-Dependent | Every seven days and after any support-system alarm or interruption. |

## Subject categories

Category describes what is being classified. It is not hierarchical and does not alter the RS
level.

| Code | Category         | Scope                                                                                                                   |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| /MAT | Material         | Chemical, mineral, component, sample, specimen, or manufactured article.                                                |
| /DEV | Device           | Instrument, mechanism, machine, assembly, equipment, or hardware system.                                                |
| /BIO | Biological       | Culture, tissue, biological specimen, organism, or biological process.                                                  |
| /LIV | Living Subject   | Animal or person under approved observation. Medical, legal, ethics, and welfare controls remain independently binding. |
| /ENV | Environment      | Room, site, atmosphere, localized field, environmental condition, or bounded area.                                      |
| /SYS | System / Process | Software, network, signal, process, procedure, or continuing operational effect.                                        |
| /UNK | Unresolved       | Temporary category used while the subject type remains unestablished.                                                   |

## Operational status codes

Status describes what is happening now. It may change without changing the standing subject
classification.

| Code | Status             | Meaning                                                                                       |
| ---- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OBS  | Observation Only   | No direct test or discretionary interaction is authorized.                                    |
| TST  | Active Test        | An approved test or experimental protocol is in progress.                                     |
| HLD  | Retained           | The subject is held under standing controls without an active test.                           |
| QTN  | Quarantine         | The subject is segregated pending medical, biological, technical, or classification review.   |
| TRN  | Authorized Transit | Controlled movement is approved under a transfer order.                                       |
| SUS  | Activity Suspended | Testing and discretionary interaction are stopped pending review.                             |
| CLS  | Closed / Archived  | Active study is complete; records and retained materials remain subject to disposition rules. |

## Subject marking format

Display the standing level first, followed by subject category and operational status.

**Standard format:** `[RS LEVEL] / [CATEGORY] / [STATUS]`

**Standard example:** `RS-4 / BIO / QTN`<br />
Condition-Dependent / Biological / Quarantine

The compact code does not replace the full label or required authorizations. A physical or
electronic label must also state:

- permanent subject or registry designator;
- current RS level and full title;
- category and operational status;
- responsible office and authorized location;
- latest and next review time; and
- applicable control-order reference.

Apply the current marking to the primary record, container or boundary, monitoring station,
transfer paperwork, and active test plan. Strike through obsolete labels, date them, and retain them
with the change record.

## Standing research control orders

- **Classification is not permission.** An RS level does not authorize entry, viewing, contact,
  testing, transport, disclosure, or destruction.
- **Highest applicable level governs.** Do not average competing indicators.
- **Unknown subjects remain in place.** Unexpected or unlabeled subjects receive RS-P and are not
  moved for convenience.
- **Transfer does not reset level.** Classification and unresolved variances follow the subject
  through storage, transport, and reassignment.
- **Normal readings do not prove downgrade.** A quiet period, inactive state, or normal instrument
  result is evidence only.
- **Preserve original records.** Append corrections with date, author, and reason. Do not overwrite
  original readings, images, or narratives.
- **Living-subject protections continue.** Classification never suspends medical care, legal
  authority, ethics review, or approved welfare requirements.
- **Report classification disagreement.** Personnel who identify a lower-than-supported level stop
  discretionary work and notify Laboratory Control.
- **Incident authority supersedes schedule.** Facility Condition and Incident Command may stop or
  redirect research activity without erasing the standing RS level.

## Separation from other Trinity authorities

The RS system operates beside, not above or below, Trinity information classification, facility
access, emergency condition, and program-order systems.

| Authority                     | What it governs                                                                     | What it does not grant                           |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| RS subject classification     | Minimum oversight, monitoring, control, and review required for the subject.        | Access, disclosure, or interaction.              |
| TL information classification | Who may receive, reproduce, or discover records and program information.            | Physical access or subject-handling authority.   |
| S security access             | Where personnel may enter and what facility functions they may physically approach. | Contact, testing, observation, or disclosure.    |
| Facility Condition            | Temporary security and emergency posture for a facility or sector.                  | Erasure or replacement of the standing RS level. |
| Program / Task Order          | Specific permission to perform a named action for a defined period and purpose.     | General authority beyond the order.              |
