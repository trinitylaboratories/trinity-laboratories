Trinity policy defines information eligibility and physical authority as separate controls. Each
record is governed by its assigned information marking.

## Interpretation rule

An information level does not establish physical authority. Physical authority does not establish
permission to receive controlled information.

- Clearance establishes eligibility.
- Compartment authorization establishes access.
- Need-to-know establishes permission.
- Physical security authority governs location and interaction.
