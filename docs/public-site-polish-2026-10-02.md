# Public site polish

## Visual thesis

Keep Trinity's blue, paper, and laboratory imagery, but give the public pages more breathing room, readable type, and a clearer path through the work.

## Content plan

Lead with the institution and the questions it helps investigate. Explain the eight approved research areas in plain English, then show the working environments and a restrained contact link. Keep technical methods and limitations available on the research pages.

## Interaction thesis

Retain the short hero entrance, gentle image reveal, and navigation underline. No new animation library, loading screen, or scroll interception. Preserve reduced-motion, keyboard, print, and no-JavaScript behavior.

## Boundaries

This is a public-corporate copy and presentation pass, not new canon. Keep all approved research titles, routes, methods, limitations, archival material, forms, and employee-access behavior. No new clients, locations, results, credentials, data collection, assets, or paid features. The fictional context remains documented in the repository.
